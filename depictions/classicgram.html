Classicgram

An unofficial Telegram client for 32-bit iPhones and iPads on iOS 6.

Classicgram is an unofficial Telegram client. It uses the Telegram API and is part of the Telegram ecosystem; it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram FZ-LLC. It brings a real, modern Telegram login to the 32-bit armv7 hardware Telegram itself dropped — iPhone 4S, iPad 2 and their peers on iOS 6 — wearing the interface Telegram for iOS had in 2013.

Highlights

  • A real login and chat list: text, photos, video, voice and round video notes, all in the 2013 bubble interface.
  • Groups, supergroups and channels; folders, archive, search and drafts.
  • 1:1 voice calls through a vendored libtgvoip, with a call log.
  • Stickers and custom emoji, with a bundled atlas for post-2012 glyphs.
  • Local notifications while backgrounded, without a jailbreak daemon.

Requires

  • A jailbroken 32-bit device (armv7) on iOS 6.0 or later.
  • Your own Telegram account — you log in with your phone number.

Tested on

iOS 6.1.3 (iPad 2). Built as a fat binary for armv7 and arm64.
