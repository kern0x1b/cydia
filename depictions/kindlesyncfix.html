Kindle Whispersync Fix

Unofficial fix that restores "Sync to Furthest Page" in the Kindle app.

Old CFNetwork attaches an empty Expect: header to the Kindle app's sync request. Amazon's legacy logging endpoint tolerates it, but the real Whispersync endpoint rejects it outright with 417 Expectation Failed, every time — so syncing fails silently with "Retrieval failed, try again later".

The tweak hooks -[NSMutableURLRequest setValue:forHTTPHeaderField:] inside the Kindle app only, and drops the header when its value is empty.

Requires

  • Jailbroken iOS with MobileSubstrate.
  • The Kindle app. Relaunch it after installing.

Tested on

iOS 6.1.3 (iPhone 4S). Builds down to iOS 5.0; if the CFNetwork bug is absent there, the hook is a no-op rather than a break.
